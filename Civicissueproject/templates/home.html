🏙 Together for a Better City

Every day we see problems around us — overflowing garbage bins, potholes, broken streetlights, unsafe roads, water issues. Too often, we just shake our heads and move on, waiting for “someone” to fix them.

But here’s the truth: change begins with us. The government cannot solve problems it doesn’t know about. By reporting civic issues, you give authorities the power to act faster and more effectively.

📢 Why Report?

  • ✔ Ensure your complaint reaches the right department.
  • ✔ Speed up problem-solving in your neighborhood.
  • ✔ Build a stronger connection between citizens and government.

🌱 Our Shared Responsibility

  • ✔ Keep our surroundings clean
  • ✔ Respect and protect public spaces
  • ✔ Take part in community efforts
  • ✔ Inspire others to take responsibility

✨ This platform is your voice. Use it. Report issues. Take action. Together, we create the city we dream of.

Civic Awareness

Awareness builds responsibility. Let’s work together to create cleaner, safer, and stronger communities.

🗑

Cleanliness is Everyone’s Duty

A clean city starts with clean habits. Dispose of waste responsibly. Do not litter. Your small act keeps your community healthy.

🚦

Road & Traffic Discipline

Obey traffic rules. Don’t block roads. Report broken signals or dangerous spots. Safe roads save lives.

💧

Water Conservation

Every drop counts. Fix leaks, avoid wastage, and report water supply issues. Conserving today secures tomorrow.

🌳

Protecting Public Spaces

Parks, footpaths, and public facilities belong to everyone. Respect and protect them—it’s your right and your responsibility.

♻

Reduce, Reuse, Recycle

Waste less, recycle more. The less we throw, the more we save—for our environment and our future.

💡

Active Citizenship

Don’t just wait for change—be part of it. Report civic issues, join local initiatives, and inspire others to act.

🔒

Safety & Security

If you see something unsafe—whether it’s a broken streetlight, open manhole, or suspicious activity—report it immediately. A safe city is a shared responsibility.

🏘

Community First

Strong communities are built when citizens care for each other. Encourage neighbors to participate in solving local issues.
